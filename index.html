Who is Kavya?

I am deeply introspective, courageous, and endlessly curious — an adventurer into inner spaces. Trained in Carnatic classical vocal since the age of five, music has always been my first language. Poetry followed — a way to hold emotions too large for conversation. Writing came next, as a practice of thinking clearly about what it means to live well.

For a long time, perfectionism and the fear of criticism kept my work private. This website is my act of courage: to share what is honest, imperfect, and of value — and to take you on a journey of feeling and healing.

About

The longer story

I started training in Carnatic classical vocal at five years old. For the next decade, I had the profound good fortune of a dedicated teacher who shaped not just my voice, but my relationship with sound. Music became the architecture through which I understood the world.

Then came engineering and technology — years of building a different kind of logic. I didn't abandon music; I simply paused. When I returned, it was with new ears. I began recording cover songs and sharing them on my YouTube channel, Kavya's Music. From 2016 onward, I performed at venues large and small with Sounds of Isha USA, a group of volunteer musicians bringing devotional music to audiences across the country. Now, I'm expanding again — learning Khayal and Dhrupad, the north Indian classical traditions, to widen my range and go deeper still.

Poetry came as a natural extension — the place where music's emotion meets language's precision. I write through metaphor about relationships, spirituality, and the beautifully complicated experience of being human. Writing has been a private practice for years. This website is where it becomes public.

I am deeply introspective. I am courageous. I am curious. I have struggled with the twin forces of perfectionism and the fear of being seen — and I am choosing, with this space, to share what is honest and of value, even when it isn't perfect. Especially then.
