Who is Kavya?

I am deeply introspective, courageous, and endlessly curious, an adventurer into inner spaces. Trained in Carnatic classical vocal since the age of five, music has always been my first language. Poetry followed, a way to express emotions too large for conversation. Writing came next, as a practice of thinking clearly about what it means to live well.

For a long time, perfectionism and the fear of criticism kept my work private. This website is my act of courage: to share what is honest, imperfect, and of value, and to take you on a journey of feeling and healing.

About

The longer story

I started training in Carnatic classical vocal at five years old. For the next decade, I had the profound good fortune of a dedicated teacher who shaped not just my voice, but my world view.

Then came engineering and technology, years of building a different kind of logic. I didn't abandon music; I simply paused. Later, I began recording cover songs and sharing them on my YouTube channel, Kavya's Music. From 2016 onward, I performed at venues large and small with Sounds of Isha USA, a group of volunteer musicians bringing devotional music to audiences across the country. Now, I'm expanding again, learning Khayal and Dhrupad, the north Indian classical traditions, to widen my range and go deeper still.

Poetry came as a natural extension, the place where music's emotion meets language's precision. I write through metaphor about relationships, spirituality, and the beautifully complicated experience of being human. Writing has been a private practice for years. This website is where it becomes public.

I am deeply introspective, courageous, and curious. I have struggled with the twin forces of perfectionism and the fear of being seen, and with this space, I am choosing to share what is honest and of value, especially when it isn't perfect.
